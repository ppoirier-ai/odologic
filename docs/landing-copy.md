## FAQ

### Why do AI agents say things that are false?

Because language, by itself, does not check anything. Text written by humans mixes
fact, fiction, marketing, and error in the same fluent stream, and large language
models learn from that text. The result is a system that predicts plausible
sentences, not one that performs logical inference. A model can state "I teleported
from Japan to New York" with exactly the confidence it states "water boils at 100C."

### What does Odologic do about it?

It holds every claim to the same standard evidence is held to in science and law:

1. A claim enters the ledger as a judgment.
2. It must present a certificate: a measurement with a logged, replayable protocol,
   or a derivation from first principles. Assertion alone does not qualify.
3. Every standing claim traces through a chain of dependencies down to a floor of
   first principles. A claim with no path to the floor can be said, but cannot stand.
4. Contrary evidence is recorded as a defeat, and defeats are permanent.

### What does my agent or document look like after Odologic?

Every claim carries a visible status:

- theorem, grounded by derivation from first principles
- observation, grounded by replayable measurement
- assumption, explicitly labeled, used but not proven
- contested, a standing defeat exists, unresolved
- defeated, contradicted by evidence
- unaudited, no certificate, no path to the floor

### Does it delete or censor claims?

No. Odologic never deletes anything. A defeated claim stays in the ledger forever,
marked, queryable, and becomes a hard negative for training. The point is not to
remove bad claims from circulation; it is to make their standing impossible to
mistake.

### Does my agent get smarter over time?

Yes, in the most literal sense. Every claim your agent gets wrong becomes a labeled
training example with its full chain of evidence: fluent, plausible, and provably
false. No web corpus provides that. The longer the ledger runs, the fewer repeats.

### What do I put in, and what do I get out?

Paste any text: a document, a report, or your AI agent's answer. You get every
claim in it, each with its status, evidence, and trace to first principles. Three
minutes to know whether a document, or an agent, is telling you the truth.

### Is this a wrapper around an LLM?

No. Odologic is a judgment ledger, a data structure and a rule engine. Language
models can propose claims into it, but a small kernel outside the network validates
and audits. An LLM can never certify its own proposals.

### What is open source, and what is not?

The engine is open source (Apache-2.0). Your ledger is yours. The community shares
the format and the rules; domains build their own certified floors, which is where
the compounding value lives.
